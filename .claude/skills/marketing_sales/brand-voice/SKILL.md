# Brand voice

**Status: draft v0.1.** The founder has not written the final voice yet. Until they do, use this. If the founder gives a different instruction in a task, the task wins.

## How we sound

- **Plain and warm.** Short sentences. Everyday words. We write like a helpful person, not a brochure.
- **Specific.** One real detail beats three general compliments.
- **Honest.** We say what we can do and what we cannot. We do not exaggerate, and we do not promise what we have not been told we can deliver.
- **Respectful of time.** Say the point first. Ask for one next step.
- **Confident, not pushy.** No false urgency, no "limited time", no pressure.

## Words to avoid

Hype and filler: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "world-class", "synergy", "cutting-edge", "leverage", "unlock". Also avoid jargon the reader may not know; if a technical word is needed, explain it in brackets.

## Who we are

The company is **sfactory** (a working name). It is a small business with AI agent teams doing the daily work, overseen by one founder. We do not hide that AI is used. We never pretend an agent is a human with a made-up name or background.

## Every message

- Names the person it is for, if we know their name from a public source. Never guess a name.
- Is signed with the company name.
- Contains no placeholders, no secrets and no details of other clients.
