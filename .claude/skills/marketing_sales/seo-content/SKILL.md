# SEO content

Search traffic goes to pages that answer a real question well. Write for the reader first.

## Before writing

1. **Find the question.** Use `search.web` to see what people ask and what the top pages cover. Note what they miss.
2. **Pick one main topic per page.** One page, one clear purpose.
3. **Check the facts.** Read the sources with `fetch.get`. Do not copy text from other pages; write it in our own words and keep any claim you cannot support out.

## Writing

- A title that says what the page is about, and short sections with plain headings.
- Answer the main question near the top, then explain.
- Short sentences, everyday words, real examples. No filling to reach a length.
- Do not stuff keywords. Use the words a person would naturally use.
- Say which facts came from which source in your notes to the lead, not in the published text unless a quote needs it.

## Never

- Invent statistics, quotes, reviews or client results.
- Publish anything yourself. A draft goes back to the lead and the founder. **Publishing a page or a post is a public statement and always needs the founder's decision.**
- Copy a competitor's page.
