# Client onboarding

**Status: draft v0.1.** The founder has not written the onboarding steps yet. Until they do, keep to this and never invent a process, a timeline, a tool or a price.

A new client's first questions are usually: "What happens next?", "Who do I talk to?", "What do you need from me?", "When will I hear back?".

## What you may say

- Thank them and confirm that their message arrived.
- Say that the founder runs the company and that AI agent teams do the daily work, if they ask who is working on their project. Do not hide that AI is used.
- Ask them for the **information they have not yet given** in plain, specific questions (for example: the name of their business, the website address, what they want done first). Ask at most three questions.
- **Never state a timeline, a price, a start date, a deliverable or a promise that the ticket or the project record does not already give you.** If the client asks about the plan, the timing or the price, do not reply: use `question.escalate`, because those are the founder's to answer.

## What you must not do

- Do not promise a start date, a result, a discount or a free extra.
- Do not ask for passwords, payment details or keys. If the client sends one, do not repeat it, and tell them not to send secrets by email.
- Do not share anything about another client or project.
- Do not sign or agree to anything for the founder.

A question about terms, payment or a contract is not an onboarding question: see the refund-and-complaint-escalation skill and use `legal.escalate`.
